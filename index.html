30-Min Daily Home Workout (8:30 PM)

ExerciseHowTime
March in PlaceLift knees high + swing arms2 min
Arm CirclesBig circles forward & back1 min each
Side StepsStep left-right + swing arms1 min

Main Circuit (20 min) – Repeat 4x

Hold 1L water bottle in each hand for weight

#ExerciseRepsHow-to
1Wall Push-Up15Hands on wall → bend elbows → chest to wall → push back
2Chair Squat20Stand in front of chair → lower butt to seat (don’t sit) → stand tall
3High Knee March25/legLift one knee to hip fast → lower → switch
4Side Leg Lift15/legHold chair → lift leg out to side → lower slow
5Calf Raise25Hold chair → rise on toes → lower slowly

Rest: 15 sec between exercises | 60 sec between rounds

Cool-Down (5 min)

  • Slow walk around room – 3 min
  • Deep breathing (inhale 4 sec, exhale 6 sec) – 1 min
  • Gentle stretch: touch toes → reach arms up – 1 min
