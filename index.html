COMPLETE 7-DAY HEALTH PLAN

Workout, Schedule, and Meal Plan for Cholesterol Health

(25F | Sri Lanka based | Works 2 PM – 11 PM)

DAILY WORKOUT STRUCTURE (30 Min Total)

Time Part Duration
0–5 min Warm-Up 5 min
5–25 min Main Circuit (Repeat 4x) 20 min
25–30 min Cool-Down Walk 5 min

Focus on completing 4 rounds (or 3 rounds during Weeks 1-2 if needed).

WARM-UP (5 Min – Every Day)

  • March in Place – 2 min (lift knees high, pump arms)
  • Arm Circles – 1 min forward + 1 min backward (small to large)
  • Side-to-Side Steps – 1 min (light steps left and right, swing arms gently)

MAIN CIRCUIT (20 Min – Repeat 4 Times)

Do each exercise $\to$ rest 15 sec $\to$ next $\to$ Rest 30 sec between rounds

බිත්තියේ තල්ලු

Wall Push-Up

**Reps:** 12–15

Hands on wall, slightly wider than shoulders $\to$ bend elbows $\to$ push back (control the movement).

පුටුවේ ස්කොට්

Chair Squat

**Reps:** 15

Lower hips back until you lightly touch the chair $\to$ stand up tall, squeezing glutes.

දණහිස ඔසවීම

High Knee March

**Reps:** 20 per leg (40 total)

Lift knee toward hip height $\to$ maintain a fast, controlled march in place. Keep core tight.

පැත්තට කකුල ඔසවීම

Side Leg Lift

**Reps:** 12 per leg

Hold chair for balance $\to$ lift leg straight out to the side $\to$ lower slowly to the ground.

කලවා ඔසවීම

Calf Raise

**Reps:** 20

Rise up high on your toes $\to$ hold for 1 sec $\to$ lower slowly. Use a wall for balance if needed.

COOL-DOWN (5 Min)

  • Slow Walk around room – 3 min
  • Deep Breathing – 1 min (Inhale 4 sec, Exhale 6 sec)
  • Gentle Stretch – 1 min (Touch toes $\to$ reach arms up high)

4-WEEK PROGRESSION

Week Reps Extra Challenge
**1** 10–12 3 rounds only
**2** 12–15 4 rounds (Goal!)
**3** 15 Add **30 sec plank** after round 4
**4** 15 Add **1 min jumping jacks** in warm-up

EXPECTED BENEFITS

  • ↑ **HDL ("good" cholesterol):** $\uparrow 4-8\ \text{mg/dL}$
  • ↓ **LDL ("bad"):** $\downarrow 10-15\ \text{mg/dL}$ (with diet)
  • ⚡️ **Energy:** $\uparrow\uparrow$ (less fatigue at 11 PM)
  • ⚖️ **Weight:** $\downarrow 1-2\ \text{kg}$ (Good start!)
  • ❤️ **Heart Health:** Stronger (Meeting AHA's $150\ \text{min/week}$ goal)

SAFETY & TIPS

  • **Wear supportive shoes** (even slippers with grip are better than bare feet).
  • **Breathe:** Exhale on effort (e.g., when pushing up or standing from the squat).
  • **Pain = Stop** (mild burn/tiredness in muscles is okay, sharp joint pain is not).
  • **Hydrate:** Drink water before, during, and after the workout.
  • **Track:** Tick daily on a fridge calendar for habit building!
